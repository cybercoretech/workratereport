GCW began as Jersey Championship Wrestling in 1999 and, as Game Changer Wrestling under Brett Lauderdale, became the hottest indie in America in the late 2010s. It mixes deathmatches, shoot-style and high-flying, and its shows often sell out within minutes. Nick Gage and Jon Moxley's deathmatch is one of its signature moments.
