Jeff and Jerry Jarrett started TNA in 2002 with weekly pay-per-views and the six-sided ring. Its X Division and the Knockouts division were its calling cards, and AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Kurt Angle gave it some of the best matches of the 2000s. After ownership changes it became Impact Wrestling, then returned to the TNA name in 2024 under Anthem Sports, with a working relationship with WWE's NXT.
