Tod Gordon started Eastern Championship Wrestling in Philadelphia in 1992. Under booker and later owner Paul Heyman it became Extreme Championship Wrestling in 1994 and changed the business with hardcore wrestling, lucha libre, Japanese influence and a cult-like home crowd at the ECW Arena. It went bankrupt in 2001, and WWE bought its assets.
