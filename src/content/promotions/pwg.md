A group of Southern California wrestlers founded PWG in 2003. Its packed shows at the American Legion Post in Reseda and the annual Battle of Los Angeles tournament became legendary. Kevin Steen and El Generico, the Young Bucks, Adam Cole and Kenny Omega were all PWG stars before they reached WWE and AEW.
