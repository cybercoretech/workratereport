Progress began in 2011 at the Camden Underworld and grew to sell out the Electric Ballroom and Alexandra Palace. It was at the center of the British boom of the 2010s, and WALTER (Gunther), Will Ospreay and Zack Sabre Jr. all held its top title or headlined its biggest shows.
