John Zandig founded CZW in 1999 as an ultraviolent successor to ECW, with light tubes, barbed wire and the Cage of Death. It also built a strong technical side; Chris Hero and Claudio Castagnoli's Kings of Wrestling started there, and Jon Moxley was a CZW world champion before WWE.
