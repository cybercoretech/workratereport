Vince McMahon bought the company from his father in 1982 and broke the territory system by going national with cable TV, Hulk Hogan and WrestleMania in 1985. After the early-1990s steroid and scandal years came the New Generation of Bret Hart and Shawn Michaels, then the Attitude Era of Steve Austin, The Rock and D-Generation X, which beat WCW in the Monday Night War. A lawsuit from the World Wildlife Fund forced the switch to the WWE name in 2002.
