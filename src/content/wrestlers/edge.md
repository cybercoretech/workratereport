Adam Copeland debuted in 1992 and teamed with his best friend Christian on the Canadian independents. The WWF signed him in 1997, and he debuted the next year. Edge & Christian won the tag titles seven times and helped make Tables, Ladders and Chairs famous.

He won the 2001 King of the Ring and, after cashing in Money in the Bank on John Cena in 2006, became one of WWE's top heels, an eleven-time world champion. He retired in 2011 because of a neck injury and was inducted into the Hall of Fame the next year.

He came back at the 2020 Royal Rumble, won the 2021 Rumble, and in 2023 joined AEW under his real name, reuniting with Christian as Cage & Cope.
