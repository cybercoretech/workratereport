The Rated-R Superstar was a ladder-match legend in the Hardys and Dudleys TLC era, then a main eventer who cashed in the first Money in the Bank briefcase in 2006. He retired in 2011 with neck injuries, returned in 2020, and joined AEW as Adam Copeland.
