Roger Barnes, a Canadian who took the name Ronnie Garvin, was a tough, no-nonsense brawler famous for his punches, hence "Hands of Stone". He worked the Mid-Atlantic, Georgia, Florida and Memphis for years.

In 1987 he beat Ric Flair for the NWA World title, and he lost it back to Flair in the main event of Starrcade '87. He also teamed with Barry Windham to win the NWA US tag titles.

In the WWF in the early 1990s he feuded with Greg Valentine and retired soon after.
