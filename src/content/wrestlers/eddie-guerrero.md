Eddie Guerrero came from one of wrestling's great families: the son of Gory Guerrero and the brother of Chavo, Mando and Héctor. He wrestled in Mexico, Japan (as Black Tiger) and ECW before WCW, where his cruiserweight matches and his Latino World Order showed off his skill.

He joined the WWF in 2000. As "Latino Heat", a crafty, lovable cheater, he won the tag titles with Chavo and then the WWE title from Brock Lesnar at No Way Out 2004, one of the most emotional title wins of the era.

He overcame addiction on the way, and he died suddenly in 2005, at 38. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2006.
