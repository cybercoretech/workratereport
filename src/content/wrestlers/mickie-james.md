Mickie James began as a valet on the independents in 1999 as Alexis Laree, trained in several camps, and gained national attention in NWA: TNA in 2002.

WWE signed her in 2005. She won the Women's title from Trish Stratus at WrestleMania 22 and became a five-time Women's champion and a Divas champion. In TNA she won the Knockouts title several times, and she has also held the NWA Women's title and worked as a country singer.

She was inducted into the TNA Hall of Fame in 2025 and is a WWE ambassador.
