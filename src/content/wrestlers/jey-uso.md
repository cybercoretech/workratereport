Joshua Fatu, the son of WWE Hall of Famer Rikishi and a member of the Anoaʻi family, was trained by his father and debuted in 2008 with his twin brother Jimmy. They arrived on WWE's main roster as the Usos in 2010.

The Usos became one of the most decorated tag teams in WWE history, and as part of Roman Reigns' Bloodline they held the tag titles for 622 days. Jey's emotional break from the Bloodline in 2023 made him a singles star.

His "Yeet" chant became one of the loudest in wrestling, and in 2025 he won the Royal Rumble and the World Heavyweight title at WrestleMania 41.
