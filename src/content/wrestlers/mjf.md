Maxwell Friedman started wrestling in 2015 on the Long Island independents. In CZW he won the World and Wired titles, and in MLW he was the first World Middleweight champion and a tag champion.

An original AEW signing, he became the company's best pure heel: smug, cowardly and brilliant on the microphone, in the mold of the 1980s NWA champions. He beat Jon Moxley for the AEW World title at Full Gear 2022 and held it for over a year, including the 60-minute Iron Man with Bryan Danielson, and he won the ROH tag titles with Adam Cole.

He has also acted, appearing in the 2023 film The Iron Claw.
