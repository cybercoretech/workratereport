Maxwell Jacob Friedman, "The Salt of the Earth", is a throwback heel in a modern company: a cocky, cowardly, brilliant talker who works like a 1980s NWA champion. His AEW World title reign included a 60-minute Iron Man classic with Bryan Danielson at Revolution 2023.
