Bob Backlund was an amateur standout at North Dakota State, trained under Eddie Sharkey, and worked the AWA and NWA before Vincent J. McMahon brought him to the WWWF in 1976.

He beat Superstar Billy Graham for the title in 1978 and held it until December 1983, a 2,135-day reign recognized as the second longest in the title's history. He lost it to the Iron Sheik, which set up Hulk Hogan's rise.

He returned in the 1990s as an unhinged heel with the crossface chickenwing and won the title again from Bret Hart at Survivor Series 1994. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2013.
