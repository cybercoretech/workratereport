Lawrence Pfohl played football for Penn State and in the CFL and USFL before wrestling in Florida in 1985. As "The Total Package" in Crockett he joined the Four Horsemen in 1987 and won the US title, which he would hold five times for a record combined length.

He finally won the WCW World title in 1991 and again in 1997, when he beat Hollywood Hogan on Nitro. In the WWF from 1993 to 1995 he was pushed as an all-American hero with the Lex Express bus tour, but never won a title.

His career ended with a spinal stroke in 2007, and since then he has become an outspoken Christian speaker.
