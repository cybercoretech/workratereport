"The Total Package" rose from the Four Horsemen to become a top star in the NWA, WCW and WWF. Luger won the WCW World title in 1991 and again in 1997, when he beat Hollywood Hogan on Nitro, and the WWF built a summer of 1993 around him with the Lex Express.
