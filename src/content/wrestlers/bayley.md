Pamela Martinez wrestled as Davina Rose on the California independents before signing with WWE in 2012. In NXT she became Bayley, the huggable superfan, and her matches with Sasha Banks in 2015, including TakeOver: Brooklyn and the first women's Iron Man match, are landmarks.

On the main roster she won the Raw and SmackDown Women's titles twice each, and her second SmackDown reign of 380 days is tied for the longest in the title's history. She and Sasha Banks were the first Women's tag champions, which made Bayley WWE's first women's Triple Crown and Grand Slam champion.

She won the 2019 Money in the Bank and the 2024 Royal Rumble, reinvented herself as a sharp heel leading Damage CTRL, and then won the WWE Women's title at WrestleMania XL.
