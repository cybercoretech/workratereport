Steve Borden was a bodybuilder who started wrestling in 1985 as one of the Blade Runners alongside the future Ultimate Warrior. In Jim Crockett Promotions his surfer look and energy made him a top babyface, and his 1988 draw with Ric Flair launched him.

He became WCW's franchise player, a multi-time world champion, and in 1996 he transformed into a silent, Crow-inspired figure who watched from the rafters for over a year before beating Hollywood Hogan at Starrcade 1997. He also had a long TNA run from 2003 to 2014, winning its world title several times.

He finally reached WWE in 2014 and was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2016, then joined AEW in 2020 and retired at Revolution 2024, undefeated in AEW, alongside Darby Allin.
