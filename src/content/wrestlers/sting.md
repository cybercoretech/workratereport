WCW's franchise. Sting broke out with a 45-minute draw against Ric Flair at the first Clash of the Champions in 1988, became a multi-time world champion, and reinvented himself as the silent, bat-wielding Crow-inspired figure of the nWo era. He finally reached WWE in 2014 and AEW in 2020, retiring at AEW Revolution 2024.
