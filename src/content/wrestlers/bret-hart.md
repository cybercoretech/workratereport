"The Hitman" called himself "the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be", and he had the matches to back it up. A five-time WWF champion, Bret carried the WWF's New Generation era, had a double-turn classic with Steve Austin at WrestleMania 13, and left in the Montreal Screwjob of 1997.
