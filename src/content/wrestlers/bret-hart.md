Bret Hart grew up in the famous Hart family of Calgary, learned in his father Stu Hart's "Dungeon" and Stampede Wrestling, and had an amateur background at Ernest Manning High School and Mount Royal College. He arrived in the WWF in 1984 and found success in the Hart Foundation with Jim Neidhart, winning the tag titles twice.

In the 1990s "The Hitman" put technical wrestling at the front of mainstream North American wrestling. He was a five-time WWF champion and two-time Intercontinental champion, and his matches with Mr. Perfect, Owen Hart, the British Bulldog, Shawn Michaels and Steve Austin are some of the best of the era. He's widely regarded as one of the greatest wrestlers ever.

After the Montreal Screwjob at Survivor Series 1997 he moved to WCW, where he won the world title twice. A concussion ended his career in 2000. He returned to WWE in 2010 and was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2006, then again with the Hart Foundation in 2019.
