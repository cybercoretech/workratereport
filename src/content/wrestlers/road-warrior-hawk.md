Mike Hegstrand was a Minneapolis bouncer, like Animal, before the two became the Road Warriors in 1983. They overpowered every team in the NWA, AWA and Japan, winning tag titles everywhere, and they were voted tag team of the year many times.

Hawk was also a sporadic singles challenger for world titles, and he headlined the first ECW November to Remember in 1993. In the WWF the Legion of Doom won the tag titles twice.

He died in 2003, at 46, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the team in 2011.
