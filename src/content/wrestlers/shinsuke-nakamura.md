Shinsuke Nakamura debuted for New Japan in 2002, mixing pro wrestling with MMA fights, and won the IWGP Heavyweight title at 23. He became a three-time IWGP champion and, as "The King of Strong Style", a defining IWGP Intercontinental champion with an eccentric, charismatic style.

He won the G1 Climax in 2011 and had classics with Tanahashi, Kota Ibushi and AJ Styles. He joined WWE in 2016, won the NXT title, and won the 2018 Royal Rumble.

On WWE's main roster he has won the Intercontinental and US titles, and he teams with Kyoki (Hiromu Takahashi) as Bakusai.
