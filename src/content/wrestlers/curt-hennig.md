Curt Hennig was the son of Larry "The Axe" Hennig and the father of Curtis Axel, and he's considered one of the greatest technical wrestlers of all time. He debuted in 1980 and won titles in Pacific Northwest Wrestling and the AWA, where he took the World title from Nick Bockwinkel in 1987.

In the WWF he became "Mr. Perfect", famous for the vignettes in which he did everything perfectly. He was a two-time Intercontinental champion and had classics with Bret Hart. In WCW he joined the Four Horsemen in 1997 and the nWo.

He died in 2003, at 44, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2007.
