Tetsuya Naito began training in 2000 and debuted for New Japan in 2006. He won the G1 Climax in 2013, but fans rejected him as a clean-cut babyface.

After his 2015 trip to Mexico he came back as the leader of Los Ingobernables de Japón and became one of the most popular wrestlers in New Japan history. He won the IWGP Heavyweight and Intercontinental titles, won the G1 three times, and at Wrestle Kingdom 14 held both titles at once.

After 21 years he left New Japan in 2025 and now works as a freelancer, mostly for Pro Wrestling Noah, where he leads Los Tranquilos de Japón and is GHC Heavyweight champion.
