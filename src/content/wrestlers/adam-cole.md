Austin Jenkins grew up in Pennsylvania and started wrestling on the East Coast independents in the late 2000s, landing in Ring of Honor in 2009. He held the CZW Junior Heavyweight and PWG World titles, setting records for the longest reign with both, and his smug, flashy heel act made him one of the most reliable main eventers on the circuit.

In ROH he became the first three-time World champion and won the 2014 Survival of the Fittest, and in New Japan he ran with Bullet Club. WWE signed him in 2017. In NXT he led the Undisputed Era and set the record for the longest NXT title reign at 403 days, with long title fights against Johnny Gargano and Tommaso Ciampa.

Cole moved to AEW in 2021, where he reunited with his old Undisputed Era partners as the Paragon. With the "Bay Bay" catchphrase and an instinct for big-match pacing, he's one of the defining independent-to-national stars of his generation.
