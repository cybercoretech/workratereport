Allen Jones debuted in 1998, had a short run in WCW's final days, and found his home in TNA. As its first X Division champion and later a multi-time NWA and TNA World champion, he was called the cornerstone of the company, and he became its first Triple Crown and Grand Slam winner. At the same time he was the inaugural ROH Pure champion.

He left TNA in 2014 and reinvented himself in New Japan as the leader of Bullet Club and IWGP Heavyweight champion. His Wrestle Kingdom 10 match with Shinsuke Nakamura is widely regarded as one of the best of the decade. WWE signed him for the 2016 Royal Rumble, and he quickly became a two-time WWE champion and one of the most consistent big-match performers in the company's history.

Known as "The Phenomenal One" for the Styles Clash and the Phenomenal Forearm, he retired from active competition and stayed with WWE as a coach and talent scout at the Performance Center.
