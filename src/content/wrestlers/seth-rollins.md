Colby Lopez, as Tyler Black, won the ROH World title and was one of the top independent stars of the late 2000s. WWE signed him in 2010, and he was the first NXT champion in 2012.

He debuted with the Shield in 2012, broke the group up with a chair in 2014, and in 2015 cashed in Money in the Bank during the WrestleMania 31 main event to become WWE champion. He's since won the Universal and World Heavyweight titles several times.

He has topped PWI's 500 three times (2015, 2019 and 2023) and was named Wrestler of the Year by Sports Illustrated in 2022.
