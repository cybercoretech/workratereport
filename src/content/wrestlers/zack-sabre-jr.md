Luke Eatwell started training at 14 at the NWA UK Hammerlock school and wrestled in Britain, then in Pro Wrestling Noah, PWG, Evolve and RevPro, where his endless counters and joint manipulation made him a cult favorite.

New Japan signed him in 2016. He won the New Japan Cup in 2018, then the G1 Climax in 2024, and soon after won the IWGP World Heavyweight title.

He leads the TMDK stable and is known for his love of tea.
