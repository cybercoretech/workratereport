Kofi Sarkodie-Mensah was born in Ghana and grew up in Massachusetts, graduating from Boston College before starting on the New England independents. WWE signed him in 2006 and he debuted in 2008 as an energetic high-flyer, winning the Intercontinental and US titles.

With Big E and Xavier Woods he formed the New Day in 2014, one of the most decorated tag teams in WWE history. In 2019 "KofiMania" swept fans up, and he beat Daniel Bryan at WrestleMania 35 to become WWE champion.

After a 20-year WWE career he joined AEW in 2026 as Kofi, teaming with Austin Creed as the New Level, and they won the AEW World Trios titles with Swerve Strickland.
