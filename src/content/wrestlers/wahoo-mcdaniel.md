Ed McDaniel played linebacker and punter in the AFL for the Houston Oilers, Denver Broncos, New York Jets and Miami Dolphins, while wrestling in the off-seasons. Of Choctaw and Chickasaw heritage, he became one of the most popular stars of the NWA territories.

He was a major star in Florida, Georgia, Texas, the AWA and above all Jim Crockett's Mid-Atlantic, where he won the NWA US title five times. His feuds and Indian strap matches with Ric Flair, Roddy Piper, Greg Valentine and Tully Blanchard drew big crowds.

He died in 2002.
