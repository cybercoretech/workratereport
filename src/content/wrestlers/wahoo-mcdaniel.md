A former pro football player and a Mid-Atlantic legend of the late 1970s and early 1980s, famous for his chops and Indian strap matches. Wahoo held the NWA United States title five times and fought Ric Flair, Roddy Piper and Greg Valentine in some of the area's biggest feuds.
