John Cena started wrestling in 1999, signed with the WWF in 2001, and trained at Ohio Valley Wrestling. He debuted against Kurt Angle on SmackDown in 2002 and broke out as a brash rapping heel.

From the mid-2000s to the mid-2010s he was WWE's franchise player, a polarizing hero who headlined WrestleMania several times and feuded with Edge, Randy Orton, CM Punk and The Rock. He was a record-setting 17-time world champion, breaking Ric Flair's record in 2025.

He wrestled part time from 2017 while building a major acting career. He retired at the end of a farewell tour in 2025 and remains with WWE as a brand ambassador.
