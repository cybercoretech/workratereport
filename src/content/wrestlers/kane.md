Glenn Jacobs started wrestling in 1992, working Smoky Mountain Wrestling and the USWA. He joined the WWF in 1995 and played Isaac Yankem, an evil dentist, and later a replacement Diesel before being repackaged as the masked Kane in 1997.

Kane won the WWF title from Steve Austin at King of the Ring 1998, the World Heavyweight and ECW titles later, and dozens of tag title reigns, including with Daniel Bryan as Team Hell No. He holds the record for the most matches in WWE history.

A libertarian Republican, he served as mayor of Knox County, Tennessee, from 2018 to 2026, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2021.
