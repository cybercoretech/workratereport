Bobby Lashley was an Army and amateur wrestling champion who debuted in WWE in 2005, winning the US title and becoming a two-time ECW World champion. He left in 2008 to fight in MMA for Strikeforce and Bellator.

Back in wrestling, he became TNA World Heavyweight champion. He returned to WWE in 2018, and with the Hurt Business he finally reached the top, winning the WWE title in 2021 and holding it for six months.

He later joined AEW, where his Hurt Syndicate stable won the AEW tag titles.
