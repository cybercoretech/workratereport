Drew Galloway started wrestling in Scotland in 2001 and was ICW's first World Heavyweight champion. WWE signed him in 2007 and, as Drew McIntyre, billed him as Vince McMahon's "Chosen One", but after an Intercontinental title run he slid down the card and was released in 2014.

He went back to the independents and TNA as Drew Galloway, won the Evolve and TNA World titles, and returned to WWE in 2017. He won the NXT title, then the 2020 Royal Rumble, and beat Brock Lesnar at WrestleMania 36 for the WWE title.

A multi-time world champion since, he's one of the great redemption stories in modern wrestling.
