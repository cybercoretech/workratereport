Jamie White, from New Zealand, joined New Japan's dojo in 2014. He went on excursion to Ring of Honor and Revolution Pro in 2016, and returned in 2017 as the cold, calculating "Switchblade".

He beat Okada at Wrestle Kingdom 13, became a multi-time IWGP world champion, won the G1 Climax in 2021, and led Bullet Club.

He moved to AEW in 2023, where he leads the Bang Bang Gang and has held the Unified World Trios titles.
