Walter Hahn from Vienna started wrestling in Germany, mainly in Westside Xtreme Wrestling, where as WALTER he was a three-time wXw Unified World champion. He also worked in Japan for Big Japan and Zero1, and in Britain and the U.S. for Progress, Evolve and PWG, building a reputation for vicious chops and hard-hitting, old-school matches.

In WWE he set the record for the longest NXT UK title reign, then, as Gunther, the record for the longest Intercontinental title reign at 666 days, leading Imperium. His 2022 match with Sheamus in Cardiff was widely called the best of the year.

He won the 2024 King of the Ring and has been a two-time World Heavyweight champion.
