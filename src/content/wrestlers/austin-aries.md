Daniel Solwold Jr. joined Ring of Honor in 2004 as part of the Generation Next faction and won the ROH tag titles with Roderick Strong. Later that year he ended Samoa Joe's historic ROH World title reign, and in 2009 he became the first man to win that title twice.

In TNA he rebuilt himself as the cocky "Greatest Man That Ever Lived". He won the X Division title, traded it in for a world title shot, and won the TNA World Heavyweight title in 2012, and between ROH and TNA held 14 championships.

He later had a run in WWE's cruiserweight division and returned to the independents and MLW, where he won the MLW National Openweight title and the Opera Cup.
