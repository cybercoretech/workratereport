Matt Hardy and his brother Jeff ran backyard shows in Cameron, North Carolina, and their own promotion, OMEGA, before the WWF signed them. As the Hardy Boyz they popularized Tables, Ladders and Chairs matches and became one of the most influential tag teams ever.

Matt also had a long singles career, from the cult-favorite "Matt Hardy Version 1" to the ECW title. In TNA in 2016 he created the surreal "Broken" Matt Hardy, with the "Final Deletion" filmed at his home, one of the most talked-about characters of the decade.

He had runs in WWE and AEW in the 2010s and 2020s, and the Hardys are back in TNA, where they have won the tag titles five times.
