Mick Foley trained under Dominic DeNucci and wrestled the territories in the 1980s. As Cactus Jack in WCW, ECW and Japanese deathmatch promotions, he lost part of his ear in a 1994 match against Vader in Germany.

In the WWF from 1996 he played three characters: the deranged Mankind, the hippie Dude Love and Cactus Jack. His Hell in a Cell match with the Undertaker, in which he was thrown off the top of the cell and then through it, became infamous. He won the WWF title three times and main-evented WrestleMania 2000.

He became a best-selling author and stand-up comedian, was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2013, and is now signed to AEW.
