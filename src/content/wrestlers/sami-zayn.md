Rami Sebei, from Montreal, debuted in 2002 as Stevie McFly before becoming El Generico, a masked "generic luchador". He won titles in IWS, PWG and ROH, and his feud with Kevin Steen became an independent classic.

WWE signed him in 2013. As Sami Zayn he won the NXT title and the Intercontinental title, and his storyline as the "Honorary Uce" of the Bloodline, ending in his betrayal of Roman Reigns in 2023, was one of the best in modern WWE.

He is the reigning Undisputed WWE champion in his second reign.
