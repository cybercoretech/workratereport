Terry Allen started wrestling in the early 1980s and broke through in Bill Watts' Mid-South, where he won the North American title. Jim Crockett Promotions brought him in, and with his looks and fire he quickly became one of its most popular stars.

He won the NWA US title twice, and his I Quit cage match with Tully Blanchard at Starrcade '85 is one of the most violent and celebrated matches of the decade. His best-of-seven series with Nikita Koloff in 1986 was another highlight.

He was being groomed for the NWA World title when a car crash in October 1986 ended his career at 27. He stayed in wrestling in non-wrestling roles.
