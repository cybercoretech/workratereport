Stephon Strickland started wrestling in 2008 as Shane Strickland and worked for MLW, Evolve, CZW and Lucha Underground, where he played Killshot. He won the MLW World Heavyweight title.

In NXT as Isaiah "Swerve" Scott he won the North American title. He joined AEW in 2022, won the tag titles with Keith Lee, and his feud with "Hangman" Adam Page made him one of AEW's top acts.

He beat Samoa Joe in 2024 to become AEW World champion, and he is one of the AEW World Trios champions with the New Level.
