Scott Rechsteiner was an amateur standout at Michigan who followed his brother Rick into wrestling. The Steiner Brothers won tag titles in WCW, the WWF and New Japan, and Scott's Frankensteiner and suplexes were far ahead of their time.

In 1998 he transformed into the massively muscled, unpredictable "Big Poppa Pump", joined the nWo, and won the WCW World title in 2000 as one of WCW's last top heels. His promos are legendary, often for the wrong reasons.

He later wrestled in WWE and TNA, and he's a four-time world champion across WCW and other promotions.
