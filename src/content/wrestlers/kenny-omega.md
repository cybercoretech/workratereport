Tyson Smith from Winnipeg spent time in WWE's developmental system before going to Japan, where his comedy and wild matches in DDT, including the Golden☆Lovers team with Kota Ibushi, made him a cult star. He also won the PWG World title.

In New Japan he won the Junior Heavyweight title, led Bullet Club, and in 2017 had the first six-star match in Observer history with Kazuchika Okada. He went on to win the IWGP Heavyweight and inaugural US titles and the 2016 G1 Climax, the first foreigner to win it.

He co-founded AEW as an executive vice president in 2019, won the AEW World title and many others, and is regarded as one of the greatest in-ring performers ever. He leads the Elite.
