Debrah Miceli started in the AWA, where she held the AWA Women's title, and in 1988 she became the first woman to win PWI's Rookie of the Year. She also had a long, successful run in Japan.

In the WWF as Alundra Blayze she won the revived Women's title three times. In December 1995 she showed up on WCW Nitro and threw the WWF Women's title into a trash can, one of the most famous moments of the Monday Night War.

She later became a champion monster truck driver and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2015. She works as an NWA producer.
