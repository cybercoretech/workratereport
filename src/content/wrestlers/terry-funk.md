Terry Funk, the son of Dory Funk Sr. and the brother of Dory Funk Jr., grew up in his father's Amarillo promotion. He won the NWA World title in 1975, and with Dory he became hugely popular in Japan.

His career lasted more than 50 years, with many retirements and comebacks. His 1989 feud with Ric Flair, ending in their I Quit match, is legendary, and in the 1990s he became the godfather of hardcore wrestling in ECW, FMW and IWA Japan, winning the ECW World title at Barely Legal in 1997, at 52.

Respected by every generation of wrestlers, he was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2009 with Dory, and he died in 2023.
