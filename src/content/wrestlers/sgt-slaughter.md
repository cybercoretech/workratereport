Robert Remus wrestled in the AWA and NWA before becoming Sgt. Slaughter. In the WWF in 1981 he was a hated drill sergeant, and in the Mid-Atlantic he won the US title twice. In 1984 he turned hero, feuding with the Iron Sheik and becoming a G.I. Joe figure.

In 1990 he returned to the WWF as an Iraqi sympathizer during the Gulf War, beat the Ultimate Warrior for the WWF title at the 1991 Royal Rumble, and headlined WrestleMania VII against Hulk Hogan.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2004 and is a WWE ambassador.
