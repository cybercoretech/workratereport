A drill-sergeant brawler who was a top star in the AWA and the WWF in the early 1980s. Slaughter's 1981 Alley Fight with Pat Patterson is legendary, and in 1991 he turned heel during the Gulf War and won the WWF title from the Ultimate Warrior.
