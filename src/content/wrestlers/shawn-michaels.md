Michael Hickenbottom started wrestling in Texas in 1984 and joined the WWF in 1988 with Marty Jannetty as the Rockers. As a singles star he won the Intercontinental title three times and had the WrestleMania X ladder match with Razor Ramon.

"Mr. WrestleMania" won his first WWF title in an Iron Man match with Bret Hart at WrestleMania XII, co-founded D-Generation X, and was at the center of the Montreal Screwjob. A back injury forced him to retire in 1998.

He returned in 2002 for a second career, won the World Heavyweight title, and had classics with the Undertaker at WrestleMania 25 and 26 before retiring. A two-time WWE Hall of Famer, he now runs NXT as WWE's senior vice president of talent development and creative.
