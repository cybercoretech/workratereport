"Mr. WrestleMania". The Heartbreak Kid went from the Rockers to the main event, winning his first WWF title in an Iron Man match with Bret Hart at WrestleMania XII and co-founding D-Generation X. After a back injury he returned in 2002 for a second career just as great, including the WrestleMania XXV classic with the Undertaker.
