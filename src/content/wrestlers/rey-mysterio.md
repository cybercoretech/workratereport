Óscar Gutiérrez, the nephew and pupil of Rey Misterio, started wrestling in Tijuana at 14 in 1989 and joined AAA in 1992. In WCW from 1996 his cruiserweight matches with Eddie Guerrero, Psicosis and Dean Malenko changed what fans expected from smaller wrestlers.

He joined WWE in 2002 and won the 2006 Royal Rumble and the World Heavyweight title at WrestleMania 22. He has also held the WWE title and many tag and midcard titles, and he's widely regarded as the greatest luchador in American wrestling history.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2023, still wrestles for WWE, and serves as AAA's on-screen general manager.
