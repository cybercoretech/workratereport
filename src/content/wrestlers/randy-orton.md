Randy Orton is a third-generation wrestler, the son of "Cowboy" Bob Orton and the grandson of Bob Orton Sr. He trained in Missouri, signed with the WWF in 2000, and debuted on the main roster in 2002.

As "The Legend Killer" in Evolution he beat Mick Foley in a brutal hardcore match and became the youngest World Heavyweight champion at SummerSlam 2004. He went on to become one of WWE's greatest stars, leading Legacy and the Viper persona, with the RKO among the most famous finishers ever.

He's a 14-time world champion, tied for the third most in history, and has wrestled for WWE for more than 20 years.
