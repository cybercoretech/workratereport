Bianca Crawford was a track athlete and fitness competitor before signing with WWE in 2016. In NXT her braid, which she cracks like a whip, and her rare power and athleticism made her a contender quickly.

After moving to SmackDown she won the 2021 Royal Rumble and beat Sasha Banks in the main event of WrestleMania 37's first night: the second women's main event at WrestleMania, and the first between two African American women. Her later 420-day Raw Women's title reign is the longest in that title's history.

Calling herself "the EST of WWE" (strongest, fastest, toughest), she's one of the cornerstones of the modern women's division.
