Sylvester Ritter played college football at Fayetteville State before turning to wrestling. In Bill Watts' Mid-South, entering with a chain and dog collar to "Another One Bites the Dust", he became the most popular wrestler in the territory and held the Mid-South North American title.

His feuds with the Fabulous Freebirds and Ted DiBiase drew huge crowds in New Orleans, and he's remembered as one of the most electrifying, charismatic performers of the early 1980s.

He joined the WWF in 1984 and was a popular midcard star through the Rock 'n' Wrestling era, later wrestling in WCW. He died in a car accident in 1998 and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2004.
