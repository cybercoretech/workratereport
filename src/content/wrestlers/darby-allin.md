Samuel Ratsch started wrestling in 2014, working for Evolve, Progress, MLW and AAA with a fearless, self-destructive style and a skater-punk look. AEW signed him in 2019.

He has won the TNT title three times, a record-tying number, and won the AEW tag titles with Sting, his mentor, who carried their partnership through Sting's retirement in 2024. He has also been AEW World champion.

Known for leaping off anything high enough to hurt and for climbing Mount Everest in 2025, he's one of the most featured wrestlers of AEW's first era.
