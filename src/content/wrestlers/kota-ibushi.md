Kota Ibushi started wrestling with DDT in 2004 and became a three-time KO-D Openweight champion. He and Kenny Omega won the KO-D tag titles twice as the Golden☆Lovers, a combined DDT record, and he later worked for New Japan, for a while holding contracts with both companies at once.

In New Japan he won the G1 Climax in 2019 and 2020 and, at Wrestle Kingdom 15, the IWGP Heavyweight and Intercontinental titles together. The Golden☆Lovers' 2018 reunion was one of the best stories of the year.

Injuries slowed him down, and in 2025 he signed with AEW, but he broke his femur in October 2025.
