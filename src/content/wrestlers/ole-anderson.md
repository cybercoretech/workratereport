Alan Rogowski served in the U.S. Army before debuting in his native Minnesota in 1967 as Rock Rogowski. In 1968 he joined Jim Crockett Promotions as Ole Anderson, Gene Anderson's storyline brother, and the Minnesota Wrecking Crew won the NWA tag titles many times.

He was a founding member of the Four Horsemen with Ric Flair, Arn Anderson and Tully Blanchard in 1985, and he was the booker and part-owner of Georgia Championship Wrestling.

He later booked for WCW and wrote a famously blunt autobiography. He died in February 2024.
