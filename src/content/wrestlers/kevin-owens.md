Kevin Steen started wrestling in Quebec at 16, in 2000. In ROH he won the World and tag titles, and in PWG he won the World title a record three times. His years teaming with and fighting El Generico are legendary.

WWE signed him in 2014. As Kevin Owens he beat John Cena in his first main-roster match, won the NXT, Intercontinental and US titles, and became Universal champion in 2016.

A hard-hitting, funny heel or a hot-tempered hero, he got Steve Austin back in the ring for a match at WrestleMania 38.
