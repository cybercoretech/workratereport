Toni Rossall, from New Zealand and Australia, started on the independents and became a star in Stardom, where she won the World of Stardom title and set a record 612-day reign as SWA World champion. She also won the Progress Women's title and the 2018 Mae Young Classic.

After a run in NXT and on SmackDown, she joined AEW in 2022 and reinvented herself in 2023 as "Timeless" Toni Storm, a black-and-white Hollywood-starlet act inspired by classic films.

She has won the AEW Women's World title several times.
