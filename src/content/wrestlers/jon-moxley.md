Jonathan Good debuted in 2004 and wrestled for HWA, wXw, Full Impact Pro, Dragon Gate USA and CZW, where he was world champion. WWE signed him in 2011 and, as Dean Ambrose, he debuted with Seth Rollins and Roman Reigns as the Shield in 2012.

In WWE he won the WWE, Intercontinental and US titles, plus Money in the Bank. He left in 2019, reclaimed the Moxley name, and in AEW became a multi-time world champion and the defining star of its early years, while also winning the IWGP US title in New Japan.

He leads the Death Riders in AEW and holds the AEW Continental title.
