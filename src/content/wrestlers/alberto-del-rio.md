José Alberto Rodríguez is the son of the Mexican legend Dos Caras and the nephew of Mil Máscaras. He competed in mixed martial arts and wrestled masked as Dos Caras Jr., winning the CMLL World Heavyweight title before signing with WWE in 2009.

Debuting in 2010 as the arrogant, aristocratic Alberto Del Rio, with a personal ring announcer and a different luxury car each week, he won the 2011 Royal Rumble and the 2011 Money in the Bank briefcase. He became the first Mexican-born world champion in WWE history, eventually holding the WWE and World Heavyweight titles twice each.

After leaving WWE he wrestled as Alberto El Patrón in AAA and Impact Wrestling, and he later promoted shows and worked as a commentator.
