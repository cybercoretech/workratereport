John Layfield played college football and briefly professionally before wrestling. In WWF he went through several gimmicks before teaming with Ron Simmons in the Acolytes, later the APA, three-time tag champions known for drinking beer, playing cards and brawling for hire.

In 2004 he became a rich, arrogant cowboy-turned-tycoon. He won the WWE title from Eddie Guerrero that summer and held it for 280 days, one of the great mid-card-to-main-event pushes of the era.

He became a long-running commentator and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2020.
