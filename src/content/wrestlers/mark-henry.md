Mark Henry was a two-time Olympian in weightlifting (1992 and 1996) and a world champion powerlifter who still holds WDFPF world records. The WWF signed him to a ten-year contract in 1996, billing him as "The World's Strongest Man".

He spent years in the midcard, from "Sexual Chocolate" to the ECW title in 2008, before his 2011 heel run made him a feared monster and World Heavyweight champion.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2018, and later worked as a coach and commentator in AEW.
