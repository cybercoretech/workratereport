Frank Goodish played college and pro football before turning to wrestling. As Bruiser Brody he became synonymous with the bloody, hardcore brawling style, and as a fiercely independent attraction he worked dates, not contracts, for World Class, the WWWF, Central States, Florida and the AWA.

In Japan he was a huge star for All Japan Pro Wrestling and also worked for New Japan. His wars with Abdullah the Butcher and his team with Stan Hansen are legendary.

Brody was killed in 1988 in a locker room in Puerto Rico, at 42. His influence on the hardcore and deathmatch styles that followed is enormous.
