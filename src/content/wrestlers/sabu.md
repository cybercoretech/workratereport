Terry Brunk was trained by his uncle Ed Farhat, the Sheik, and started on the independents before becoming one of the defining stars of ECW. Billed as "homicidal, suicidal, genocidal", he threw himself through tables and onto barbed wire with no regard for his body.

He won the ECW World title twice and the NWA World title once, and his feud with Taz, settled at ECW's first pay-per-view in 1997, is legendary. He also worked in Japan, WCW, TNA and WWE's ECW revival.

Widely regarded as a pioneer of hardcore wrestling, he had his final match in 2025 and died that May.
