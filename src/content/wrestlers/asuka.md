Kanako Urai started wrestling in 2004, stepped away in 2006, and came back in 2007 as Kana, a hard-kicking freelancer who worked for JWP, NEO, Wave, Reina, Smash and Wrestling New Classic and built a cult following.

WWE signed her in 2015, the first Japanese woman the company had signed in over two decades, and as Asuka she ran through NXT on an undefeated streak of more than 900 days, including a record-setting NXT Women's title reign. On the main roster she won the first women's Royal Rumble in 2018, and her streak finally ended against Charlotte Flair at WrestleMania 34.

Since then she has won the Raw, SmackDown and Women's tag titles and the 2020 Money in the Bank, completing a women's Grand Slam with her stiff kicks and mind games intact.
