Steven Haworth started on the American independents in 1999 as Nigel McGuinness and joined Ring of Honor in 2003. He won the Pure title in 2005 and held it for a then-record 350 days, then unified it with Bryan Danielson's World title in Liverpool.

He won the ROH World title in 2007 and held it for over a year, known for his lariat and his matches with Danielson, Tyler Black and KENTA. He also wrestled for Pro Wrestling Noah, and in TNA as Desmond Wolfe.

Health issues ended his first career, and he became a commentator for WWE's NXT and NXT UK, then AEW, where he calls Collision and made a ring return against Danielson.
