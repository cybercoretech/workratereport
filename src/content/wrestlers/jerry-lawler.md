Jerry Lawler was a DJ and an artist before becoming the top star of his hometown Memphis territory in the 1970s. In the CWA he won the AWA Southern Heavyweight title 57 times and the CWA World title, and he booked the territory and its successor, the USWA.

His feud with the comedian Andy Kaufman, including the famous 1982 Late Night with David Letterman appearance, brought Memphis wrestling national attention. He beat Curt Hennig for the AWA World title in 1988.

He joined the WWF in 1992 and became one of its best-known commentators for two decades. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2007.
