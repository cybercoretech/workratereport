Windham Rotunda was a third-generation wrestler: the grandson of Blackjack Mulligan and the son of Mike Rotunda. He debuted in WWE's developmental system in 2009 and appeared on the main roster in 2010 as Husky Harris of the Nexus.

Reinvented in NXT as Bray Wyatt, the leader of the Wyatt Family, he feuded with John Cena, the Undertaker and Randy Orton, and won the WWE title in 2017. In 2019 he created the Firefly Fun House and his alter ego The Fiend, won the Universal title twice, and became one of the most creative storytellers in wrestling.

Released in 2021, he returned in 2022 to a huge reaction, but he died of a heart attack in August 2023 at 36.
