Joe Anoaʻi, the son of Sika of the Wild Samoans, played football at Georgia Tech and briefly in the NFL and CFL before signing with WWE in 2010. He debuted with the Shield in 2012 and won his first WWE title in 2015.

After years of fans resisting him as a babyface, he turned heel in 2020 as "the Tribal Chief", leading the Bloodline with Paul Heyman, and his 1,316-day Universal title reign is the longest in that title's history and one of the longest in WWE. He headlined WrestleMania more often than anyone of his era.

He lost the title to Cody Rhodes at WrestleMania XL, and is now World Heavyweight champion.
