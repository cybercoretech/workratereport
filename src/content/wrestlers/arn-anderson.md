Martin Lunde took the name Arn Anderson as Ole Anderson's storyline nephew and became the Enforcer of the Four Horsemen alongside Ric Flair, Ole and Tully Blanchard. Widely regarded as one of the greatest tag team wrestlers ever, he won the NWA/WCW tag titles several times and the WWF tag titles with Tully as the Brain Busters.

He was also a respected singles wrestler and a four-time NWA/WCW World Television champion, the title he called his "world title". His spinebuster and his dry, believable promos made him the wrestler's wrestler of the Crockett and WCW years.

After a neck injury ended his career in 1997, he handed his spot in the Horsemen to Curt Hennig in a famous emotional promo, became a long-time WWE producer, and joined AEW in 2019. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the Four Horsemen in 2012.
