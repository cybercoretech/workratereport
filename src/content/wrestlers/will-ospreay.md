Will Ospreay started on the British independents and became a star in Progress and Revolution Pro. New Japan signed him in 2016, and he won the Best of the Super Juniors twice and the IWGP Junior Heavyweight title.

He grew into a heavyweight, won the 2021 New Japan Cup and the IWGP World Heavyweight title, led the United Empire, and became one of the most five-star-rated wrestlers in Observer history.

He joined AEW full time in 2024, headlined All In at Wembley, won the International title, and is now AEW World champion. He also produces for the women's promotion Pro-Wrestling: EVE.
