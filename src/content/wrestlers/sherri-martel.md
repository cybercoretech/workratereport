Sherri Russell trained in South Carolina and started in Mid-South. In the AWA she won the Women's title three times, and in the WWF she won the Women's title in 1987.

"Sensational Sherri" became one of the best managers ever, guiding Randy Savage as "Queen Sherri", Ted DiBiase, Shawn Michaels, for whom she sang "Sexy Boy", and Harlem Heat and Ric Flair in WCW. She was one of the most physical, committed managers of the era.

She died in 2007 and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2006.
