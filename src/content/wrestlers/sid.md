Sid Eudy played softball and worked as Lord Humongous in Memphis before WCW signed him in 1989. In the Skyscrapers and the Four Horsemen he was pushed as a monster, and in the WWF in 1992 he main-evented WrestleMania VIII against Hulk Hogan.

As Sycho Sid he beat Shawn Michaels for the WWF title at Survivor Series 1996 and won it again in 1997, main-eventing WrestleMania 13. Back in WCW he won the world title twice.

A gruesome leg injury in 2001 ended his main career. He died in August 2024.
