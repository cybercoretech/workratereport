Jamar Shipman started in Ring of Honor and in TNA, where he won the X Division title and became a fan favorite with his Randy Savage-inspired "Black Machismo" act.

Back in ROH he became the record-setting, longest-reigning ROH World Television champion, then a two-time ROH World champion with the most combined days as champion in the company's history.

He joined AEW in 2021 and also works for ROH, where he leads the Lethal Twist.
