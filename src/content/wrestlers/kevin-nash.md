Kevin Nash played college basketball before wrestling for WCW from 1990 to 1993 in a run of short-lived gimmicks, including Oz and Vinnie Vegas. In the WWF as Diesel he won the World, Intercontinental and tag titles, the Triple Crown, and his 358-day WWF title reign was the longest of the 1990s.

In 1996 he and Scott Hall jumped to WCW as the Outsiders, starting the nWo and turning the Monday Night War. He won the WCW World title several times and ended Goldberg's streak at Starrcade 1998.

He later wrestled in WWE and TNA and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame twice: on his own in 2015 and with the nWo in 2020.
