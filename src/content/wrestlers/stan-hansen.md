Stan Hansen was a college football player at West Texas State who started wrestling in 1973. He became known for his lariat and a stiff style he blamed on poor eyesight, and for his cowboy act with a bullrope and a chewing-tobacco snarl.

In Japan he became more famous and revered than in America: a top star for New Japan and then, for decades, All Japan, where he won the Triple Crown several times and had wars with Jumbo Tsuruta, Mitsuharu Misawa and Kenta Kobashi. His team with Bruiser Brody is legendary.

In the U.S. he held the AWA World title in 1985–86. He's in many halls of fame, including the WWE Hall of Fame (2016).
