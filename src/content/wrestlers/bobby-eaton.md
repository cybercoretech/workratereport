Bobby Eaton started wrestling in 1976 and worked across the South: NWA Mid-America, the CWA, Mid-South, World Class, Jim Crockett Promotions, WCW and Smoky Mountain Wrestling.

Managed by Jim Cornette, he was the constant in the Midnight Express, first with Dennis Condrey and then with Stan Lane. The team's feuds with the Rock 'n' Roll Express and the Road Warriors are among the best tag wrestling of the decade. He was a three-time NWA/WCW tag champion and also won the WCW Television title.

Respected by peers as one of the smoothest workers ever, he wrestled until 2015 and died in 2021.
