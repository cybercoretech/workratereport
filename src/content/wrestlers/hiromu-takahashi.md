Hiromu Takahashi debuted for New Japan in 2010 and spent three years as a Young Lion. In 2014 he was sent to CMLL in Mexico as the masked Kamaitachi, where his feud with Dragon Lee became a fan favorite.

Back in New Japan with Los Ingobernables de Japón, he became the ace of the junior division: a multi-time IWGP Junior Heavyweight champion and a three-time Best of the Super Juniors winner, known for reckless, high-speed matches and a manic personality. A 2018 neck injury cost him more than a year.

After a sixteen-year New Japan career he moved to WWE in 2026 as Kyoki, teaming with Shinsuke Nakamura as Bakusai.
