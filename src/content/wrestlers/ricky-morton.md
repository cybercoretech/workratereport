Ricky Morton, the son of a Memphis wrestling referee, started in the Continental Wrestling Association and formed the Rock 'n' Roll Express with Robert Gibson in 1983. With their bandanas and teen-idol appeal they were called "the consummate babyface tag team".

Morton was the master of selling and the hope spot, and the Express won the NWA World tag titles several times in Jim Crockett Promotions. Their feuds with the Midnight Express, the Four Horsemen and others drew big crowds in Mid-South, Crockett, Smoky Mountain and beyond, and they also had stints in the WWF, ECW and Japan.

Still wrestling into his late sixties, he was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with Gibson in 2017.
