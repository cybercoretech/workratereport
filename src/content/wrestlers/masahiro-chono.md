Masahiro Chono debuted for New Japan in 1984 and rose as one of the Three Musketeers. He won the G1 Climax five times, a record, and the NWA World title in 1992.

From 1994 he became New Japan's top heel with a yakuza-inspired, black-clad persona, and he led nWo Japan, Team 2000 and Black New Japan. He also appeared in WCW as part of the nWo.

He left New Japan after 26 years in 2010 and has since worked in other promotions and as a TV personality.
