The biggest star of the 1980s boom. Hogan beat the Iron Sheik for the WWF title in 1984, headlined the first WrestleMania and slammed André the Giant at WrestleMania III. In 1996 he reinvented himself as "Hollywood" Hogan, leader of the nWo in WCW.
