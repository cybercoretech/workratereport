Terry Bollea trained in Florida in 1977 and wrestled as Terry Boulder and Sterling Golden before the WWF named him Hulk Hogan in 1979. He became a star in the AWA and in Japan, where he won New Japan's first IWGP League in 1983.

He beat the Iron Sheik for the WWF title in January 1984, and "Hulkamania" carried the WWF's national expansion: he headlined the first WrestleMania and slammed André the Giant at WrestleMania III. He was a six-time WWF/WWE champion.

In 1996 he turned heel at Bash at the Beach and led the nWo as "Hollywood" Hogan, the centerpiece of WCW's Monday Night War run. He later wrestled in WWE again and in TNA. He died in July 2025.
