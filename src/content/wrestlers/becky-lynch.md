Rebecca Quin began training in Ireland in 2002 and wrestled across Europe and North America as Rebecca Knox. A severe head injury in 2006 kept her out for years, and she returned to sign with WWE in 2013.

She was the inaugural SmackDown Women's champion in 2016, but the change came in 2018: her heel turn on Charlotte Flair at SummerSlam turned into "The Man", and fans adopted her as their hero. She won the 2019 Royal Rumble and won both women's titles in the first women's main event of WrestleMania.

A multi-time champion on Raw and SmackDown and a Women's tag champion, she has been one of WWE's top stars of the 2020s.
