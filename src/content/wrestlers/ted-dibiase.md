Ted DiBiase was the stepson of wrestler "Iron" Mike DiBiase and a respected technical wrestler in Mid-South and Georgia, holding 30 titles in his career. His betrayal of the Junkyard Dog made him the top heel in Mid-South.

In the WWF from 1987 he became "The Million Dollar Man", the rich villain who tried to buy the WWF title from André the Giant in 1988, created his own Million Dollar title, and paid people to humiliate themselves. He won the tag titles with Irwin R. Schyster as Money Inc.

He managed the Million Dollar Corporation and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2010. His sons Ted Jr. and Brett also wrestled.
