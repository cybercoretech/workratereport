Gail Kim wrestled on the Canadian and American independents before WWE signed her in 2002. She won the WWE Women's title in her very first match. WWE released her in 2004, and she joined TNA in 2005 as the valet for America's Most Wanted.

In 2007 she became the first TNA Knockouts champion, and her matches with Awesome Kong showed what women's wrestling on national TV could be. She went on to win the Knockouts title a record seven times.

She retired in 2019 and became a TNA producer, and she was the first woman inducted into the TNA Hall of Fame.
