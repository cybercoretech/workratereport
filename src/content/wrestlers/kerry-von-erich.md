Kerry Adkisson, Fritz Von Erich's son, spent his first 11 years in his father's World Class Championship Wrestling. With brothers Kevin, David and Mike, he made the Von Erichs Texas royalty, and their feud with the Fabulous Freebirds made World Class a national hit.

Months after David's death, Kerry beat Ric Flair for the NWA World title in front of a huge crowd at Texas Stadium. He also won the WCWA World title four times and held 40 championships in all. A 1986 motorcycle accident cost him his right foot, which he kept secret while returning to the ring.

As the Texas Tornado in the WWF he won the Intercontinental title in 1990. He died in 1993, one of several Von Erich tragedies, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the family in 2009.
