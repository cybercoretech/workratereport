"The Modern Day Warrior" was World Class Championship Wrestling's golden boy. Kerry won the NWA World title from Ric Flair in front of more than 40,000 fans at the 1984 David Von Erich Memorial Parade of Champions, and later held the WWF Intercontinental title as the Texas Tornado.
