Dave Bautista started wrestling late, at 30, trained at Ohio Valley Wrestling, and reached WWE's main roster in 2002. As "The Animal" in Evolution he was the muscle behind Triple H, until he turned on him in 2005.

He won the 2005 Royal Rumble and beat Triple H at WrestleMania 21 for the World Heavyweight title, and his first reign of 282 days is the longest in that title's history. He went on to win the WWE title twice and the World Heavyweight title four times, and won the Rumble again in 2014.

One of the most popular stars of the Ruthless Aggression era, he became a major film actor, from Guardians of the Galaxy to Dune, and retired from wrestling after facing Triple H at WrestleMania 35 in 2019.
