Tully Blanchard, the son of Texas promoter Joe Blanchard, started in Southwest Championship Wrestling and joined Jim Crockett Promotions in the mid-1980s, where he won the TV and US titles.

He was a founding member of the Four Horsemen with Ric Flair and Arn and Ole Anderson, and with Arn he won the NWA tag titles. As the Brain Busters in the WWF they won the tag titles in 1989.

He later became a Christian minister and managed FTR in AEW. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the Four Horsemen in 2012.
