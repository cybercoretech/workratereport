The original Four Horseman with the slingshot suplex and the slickest heel instincts in the Mid-Atlantic. Blanchard's 1985 "I Quit" cage match with Magnum T.A. is one of the most violent and celebrated matches of the decade, and he and Arn Anderson won the WWF tag team titles as the Brain Busters in 1989.
