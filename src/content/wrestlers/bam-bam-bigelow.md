Scott Bigelow, with the flame tattoo across his bald head, was praised by Bret Hart as possibly the best working big man in the business. He debuted in the mid-1980s, made his name quickly in New Japan, and signed with the WWF in 1987.

He headlined seven pay-per-views, including the first Survivor Series and WrestleMania XI against NFL legend Lawrence Taylor. In ECW he won the World Heavyweight and Television titles, and in WCW he won the tag titles as part of the Jersey Triad.

He also held tag gold in Japan with Big Van Vader. Bigelow died in 2007 at 45, remembered as the most agile big man of his era.
