Page Falkinburg wrestled three matches in 1979, quit, and came back as a manager in the AWA in 1988 before joining WCW as a manager in 1991. He started wrestling full time in his late thirties, which makes him one of wrestling's great late-bloomer stories.

His feuds with Randy Savage and the nWo made him one of WCW's most popular stars, and he won the WCW World title three times starting in 1999.

After WCW he wrestled in the WWF and TNA and made appearances in AEW. Outside the ring, his DDP Yoga program helped revive the careers of Jake Roberts and Scott Hall. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2017.
