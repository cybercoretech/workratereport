Harley Race was one of the toughest men ever to wrestle. He survived a serious car accident as a young man, won tag titles in the AWA, and was the first NWA United States champion.

He won the NWA World Heavyweight title eight times between 1973 and 1984, defending it across the territories and around the world, and his loss to Ric Flair at the first Starrcade passed the torch to the next era. He later wrestled in the WWF as "King" Harley Race and managed in WCW.

He ran a promotion and a respected training school in Missouri. He's one of the few men in the WWE, NWA, WCW, Professional Wrestling and Observer halls of fame. He died in 2019.
