Ashley Fliehr is Ric Flair's daughter. She first appeared in wrestling as a child alongside her father in WCW in 1993, and she began training with WWE in 2012. In NXT she won the Women's title and was named PWI's Rookie of the Year for 2014.

On the main roster she became the most decorated woman in WWE history, a record 14-time world champion across the Divas, Raw, SmackDown and WWE Women's titles. Her rivalries with Sasha Banks, Becky Lynch, Asuka and Rhea Ripley include several historic firsts, among them the first women's Hell in a Cell and a WrestleMania main event.

She won the 2020 and 2025 Royal Rumbles, and her athleticism, figure-four and Flair-inherited showmanship make her one of the defining women of the modern era.
