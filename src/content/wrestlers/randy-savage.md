"Macho Man" was one of the most intense performers wrestling has ever seen. Savage had his famous Intercontinental title classic with Ricky Steamboat at WrestleMania III, won the WWF title at WrestleMania IV and again at WrestleMania VIII, and later won the WCW World title four times.
