Randy Poffo played minor league baseball in the St. Louis Cardinals and Cincinnati Reds systems before wrestling. With his father Angelo and brother Lanny he ran International Championship Wrestling in Kentucky, then worked Memphis, where he feuded with Jerry Lawler.

In the WWF "Macho Man" became one of the most intense, flamboyant performers ever. He won the Intercontinental title, had a landmark match with Ricky Steamboat at WrestleMania III, won the WWF title at WrestleMania IV and again at WrestleMania VIII, and formed the Mega Powers with Hulk Hogan.

In WCW he won the world title four times. He died in 2011, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2015.
