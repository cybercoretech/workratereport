Nick Massie started wrestling with his older brother Matt on the California independents in 2004, after building a ring in their backyard. Nick, the faster high-flyer, and Matt became the Young Bucks, champions in PWG, ROH and New Japan's junior tag division.

Their Being The Elite series and DIY merchandise made them independent stars, and with Cody Rhodes and Kenny Omega they launched All In in 2018 and co-founded AEW in 2019 as executive vice presidents.

With Matt, he's a record four-time AEW World tag team champion and the current champion.
