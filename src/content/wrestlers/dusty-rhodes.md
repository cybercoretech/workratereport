Virgil Runnels, from Austin, Texas, was a plumber's son who wrestled first as a heel, in the Outlaws with Dick Murdoch, before his Florida turn made him the American everyman. His promos, including "Hard Times", are among the most famous ever.

A three-time NWA World champion and the Four Horsemen's great rival, he was also the creative mind of Jim Crockett Promotions in the 1980s, behind Starrcade, WarGames and the Great American Bash. He worked in the WWF in the polka dots from 1989 to 1991.

He later trained a generation at WWE's NXT and Performance Center. He died in 2015, and his sons Dustin and Cody became stars too. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2007.
