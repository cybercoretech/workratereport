"The American Dream" was the common man's hero: a plumber's son with a lisp, a strut and promos nobody could match. A three-time NWA World champion and the Four Horsemen's great rival, he was also the creative mind behind Starrcade, WarGames and much of 1980s Crockett booking, and later a mentor to a generation at the WWE Performance Center.
