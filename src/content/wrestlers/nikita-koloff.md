Nelson Simpson was a college football player who was turned into the "Russian Nightmare" Nikita Koloff in 1984 and billed from the Soviet Union. With Ivan Koloff and Krusher Khruschev he won the NWA tag and six-man titles.

His 1986 best-of-seven US title series with Magnum T.A. was a huge success. After Magnum's career-ending car crash, Nikita turned babyface to team with Dusty Rhodes, and he was part of the first WarGames against the Four Horsemen in 1987. He also held the NWA TV and UWF TV titles.

He retired in 1992 and became a Christian minister. He was inducted into the NWA Hall of Fame in 2008.
