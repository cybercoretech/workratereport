"The Russian Nightmare" was Jim Crockett Promotions' monster heel, then one of its biggest babyfaces after turning to help Dusty Rhodes and Magnum T.A. in 1986. He won the NWA United States title in a best-of-seven series with Magnum and fought the Horsemen in the first WarGames.
