Mercedes Varnado started wrestling in 2010 on the independents, mainly for Chaotic Wrestling, and signed with WWE in 2012. As Sasha Banks, "The Boss", she was one of NXT's Four Horsewomen.

On the main roster she won the Raw and SmackDown Women's titles several times and the Women's tag titles, main-evented a pay-per-view in the first women's Hell in a Cell, and main-evented WrestleMania 37's first night against Bianca Belair.

She left WWE in 2022 and, as Mercedes Moné, won the IWGP Women's title in New Japan. She joined AEW in 2024, became TBS champion and collected titles worldwide, and is now AEW Women's World champion.
