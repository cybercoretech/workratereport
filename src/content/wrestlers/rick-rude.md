Rick Rood debuted in 1982 and worked Memphis, Florida, Mid-South and World Class, where he won the WCCW World title. His physique, his insults to the crowd, and the airbrushed tights he wore made him one of the best heels of his era.

In the WWF he beat the Ultimate Warrior for the Intercontinental title at WrestleMania V. In WCW he won the US title and three WCW International World titles, and his 1992 Iron Man match with Ricky Steamboat is a classic.

A back injury ended his career in 1994. He had a final match in 1997 and was part of D-Generation X and the nWo, famously appearing on Raw and Nitro on the same night. He died in 1999, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2017.
