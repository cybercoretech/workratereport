"Rowdy" Roddy Piper was the greatest heel talker of the Rock 'n' Wrestling era. He was the villain opposite Hogan at the first WrestleMania, the host of Piper's Pit, a star in the NWA's Mid-Atlantic and Georgia territories before that, and later Intercontinental and WCW US champion.
