Roderick Toombs, a Canadian billed from Glasgow with a kilt and bagpipes, started young and worked across the territories. In Mid-Atlantic and Georgia he was a top star, and his dog collar match with Greg Valentine at the first Starrcade is legendary.

In the WWF from 1984 he hosted Piper's Pit and was the villain opposite Hulk Hogan in the main event of the first WrestleMania. He became a hero later and won the Intercontinental title at WrestleMania VIII, and in WCW he won the US title and feuded with Hollywood Hogan.

He starred in the film They Live in 1988. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2005 and died in 2015.
