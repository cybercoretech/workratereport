Ettore Ewen played college football at Iowa and was a USA Powerlifting champion before signing with WWE in 2009. He arrived on the main roster as Dolph Ziggler's muscle and won the Intercontinental title in 2013.

The New Day turned a struggling act into a phenomenon. With Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods he became an eight-time tag team champion across Raw and SmackDown, with the longest reigns of the era.

His singles moment finally came in 2021, when he cashed in Money in the Bank to become WWE champion. A neck fracture in 2022 ended his in-ring career, and he announced his retirement in 2025, staying with WWE as an analyst and spokesman.
