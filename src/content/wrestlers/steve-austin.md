Steve Austin was born Steven Anderson and later took his stepfather's name, Williams. He trained in Texas, worked in the USWA and World Class, and as "Stunning" Steve Austin in WCW won the US and TV titles and the tag titles as half of the Hollywood Blonds with Brian Pillman.

After WCW fired him, he cut furious promos in ECW and joined the WWF in 1996 as the Ringmaster, before reinventing himself as "Stone Cold". His feud with Vince McMahon drove the WWF to victory in the Monday Night War, and he won the WWF title six times and headlined several WrestleManias.

Neck injuries forced him to retire in 2003, and he became a TV host and actor. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2009 and returned for a match with Kevin Owens at WrestleMania 38.
