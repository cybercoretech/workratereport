Nick Bockwinkel, the son of wrestler Warren Bockwinkel, debuted in 1954 and spent his first two decades as a journeyman babyface in California, Hawaii, Texas, Georgia and the Pacific Northwest.

In 1970 he joined the AWA and reinvented himself as an articulate, condescending heel with Bobby Heenan. He won the AWA World title four times between 1975 and 1987, defending it in long, technical matches, and was one of the best talkers of his time.

He wrestled for 34 consecutive years, later served as a WCW commissioner and a respected Cauliflower Alley Club president, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2007. He died in 2015.
