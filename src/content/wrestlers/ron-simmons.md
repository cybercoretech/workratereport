Ron Simmons was an All-American at Florida State and played in the NFL, CFL and USFL before wrestling. In Jim Crockett Promotions and WCW he formed Doom with Butch Reed, winning the tag titles, and in 1992 he beat Vader for the WCW World title.

In the WWF he became Faarooq, leader of the Nation of Domination, then teamed with Bradshaw in the Acolytes and the APA, winning the tag titles three times. His single word, "Damn!", became a catchphrase.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2012.
