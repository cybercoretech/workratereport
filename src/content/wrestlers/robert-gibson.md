Robert Gibson grew up in a wrestling family and started young. He formed the Rock 'n' Roll Express with Ricky Morton in Memphis in 1983, and they became the most popular babyface tag team in the country.

The Express won the NWA World tag titles multiple times in Jim Crockett Promotions, and Gibson, the solid, technical half of the team, also won singles titles. Their feuds with the Midnight Express, the Andersons and the Horsemen are tag team classics.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with Morton in 2017.
