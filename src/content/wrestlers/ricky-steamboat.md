Richard Blood started in the AWA in 1976 and moved to Jim Crockett's Mid-Atlantic in 1977, where his feud with Flair began and he won the US title four times and many tag titles.

In the WWF from 1985, "The Dragon" was injured by Randy Savage in a famous angle and beat him for the Intercontinental title at WrestleMania III. He returned to Crockett in 1989 and won the NWA World title from Flair at Chi-Town Rumble, then had the Clash VI and WrestleWar classics. He's regarded as one of the most influential wrestlers ever.

A back injury ended his full-time career in 1994. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2009, worked as a WWE producer, had a ring return in 2022, and is signed to AEW.
