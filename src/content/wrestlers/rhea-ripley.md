Demi Bennett from Adelaide started wrestling under her real name in 2013 and joined WWE in 2017 for the first Mae Young Classic. She was the first NXT UK Women's champion and later NXT Women's champion.

With the Judgment Day she became "Mami", won the 2023 Royal Rumble, and beat Charlotte Flair at WrestleMania 39. She has held the Women's World title twice, and her first 380-day reign is tied with Bayley for the longest, and she has twice held the WWE Women's title.

Her intimidating style and strength have made her one of the defining women of the 2020s.
