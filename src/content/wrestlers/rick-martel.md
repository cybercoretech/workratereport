Richard Vigneault from Quebec City wrestled in Canada, New Zealand and the Pacific Northwest before the AWA, where he beat Jumbo Tsuruta for the World title in 1984 and held it for well over a year.

In the WWF he won the tag titles with Tito Santana as Strike Force, then turned heel in 1989 and became "The Model", spraying opponents with his Arrogance cologne.

He later won the WCW Television title and retired in the late 1990s to train wrestlers and work in television.
