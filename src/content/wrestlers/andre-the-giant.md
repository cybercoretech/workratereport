André Roussimoff was born in France in 1946 and, because of gigantism, grew to well over seven feet. He started wrestling in Europe in 1966 and moved to North America in 1971, and from 1973 he toured the world as "The Eighth Wonder of the World": the unbeatable attraction every territory wanted on its biggest card.

During the 1980s boom he turned heel with Bobby Heenan, and his WrestleMania III main event against Hulk Hogan, with Hogan's bodyslam in front of a vast crowd at the Pontiac Silverdome, became one of the most famous moments in wrestling history. In 1988 he beat Hogan for the WWF title on the first Main Event, before handing it to Ted DiBiase.

Also remembered as Fezzik in The Princess Bride, André died in 1993 and was the first inductee into the WWF Hall of Fame.
