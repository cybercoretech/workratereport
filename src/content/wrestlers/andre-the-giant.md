"The Eighth Wonder of the World" was wrestling's most famous attraction worldwide for nearly two decades. His heel turn and WrestleMania III main event against Hulk Hogan, before more than 90,000 people at the Pontiac Silverdome, remains one of the most famous matches in the business.
