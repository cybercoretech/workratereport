Michael Seitz debuted in 1977 and founded the Fabulous Freebirds in 1979 with Terry Gordy, later joined by Buddy Roberts and Jimmy Garvin. They were the first group to walk to the ring to rock music, and they wrestled across Mid-South, World Class, the WWF and WCW.

Their feud with the Von Erichs, which started with the Christmas 1982 cage-door turn, made World Class a phenomenon, and the Freebirds held tag and six-man titles in World Class, Mid-South and WCW.

Hayes moved behind the scenes and became one of WWE's longest-serving producers and writers. The Freebirds were inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2016.
