Brittany Baker started wrestling on the independents while studying dentistry, and she is a practicing dentist, hence the "D.M.D." in her ring name. In 2019 she became the first woman signed by AEW.

Her turn into an arrogant heel, with Rebel and later Jamie Hayter at her side, made her one of the most popular acts in the company. Her bloody Lights Out match with Thunder Rosa in March 2021 was the first women's main event of Dynamite.

She won the AEW Women's World title at Double or Nothing 2021 and held it for 290 days, and she remained one of AEW's most recognizable women.
