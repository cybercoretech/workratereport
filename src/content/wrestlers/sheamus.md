Stephen Farrelly from Dublin started on the European independents in 2002 and signed with WWE in 2007. He debuted on ECW in 2009 and was WWE champion by the end of the year.

"The Celtic Warrior" won the 2010 King of the Ring, the 2012 Royal Rumble, and the World Heavyweight title at WrestleMania XXVIII. He's a four-time world champion, held the tag titles with Cesaro, and won the US and Intercontinental titles.

His hard-hitting 2022 match with Gunther in Cardiff was one of the best of the year.
