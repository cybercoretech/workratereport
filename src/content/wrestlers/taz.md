Peter Senerchia began wrestling in 1987 and joined Eastern Championship Wrestling in 1993 as the Tazmaniac. After a neck injury in 1995 he became Taz, a believable, menacing shoot-fighter with his Tazmission and endless suplexes.

He won the ECW World title and the FTW title he created, and his feud with Sabu peaked at ECW's first pay-per-view. He joined the WWF in 2000, beating Kurt Angle in his debut at the Royal Rumble.

He became a long-time commentator in WWE and TNA and now calls AEW Dynamite, where he has managed his son Hook.
