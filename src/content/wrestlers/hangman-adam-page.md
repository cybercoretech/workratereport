Stephen Woltz started wrestling in 2008 and spent seven years in Ring of Honor from 2011. Joining Bullet Club in 2016 brought out the "Hangman" persona, and with the Young Bucks he won the ROH Six-Man tag titles and worked for New Japan.

An original AEW signing in 2019, he won the AEW tag titles with Kenny Omega. His slow-burn story of self-doubt, leaving and rejoining the Elite, ended with him beating Omega for the AEW World title at Full Gear 2021, one of the most emotional moments in AEW history.

He has stayed one of AEW's top stars since, with a celebrated feud with Swerve Strickland.
