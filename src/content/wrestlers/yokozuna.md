Rodney Anoaʻi was a member of the Anoaʻi family, the nephew of the Wild Samoans. He wrestled as Kokina in the AWA, Mexico and New Japan before the WWF repackaged him in 1992 as Yokozuna, a sumo champion.

He won the 1993 Royal Rumble and beat Bret Hart for the WWF title at WrestleMania IX, then headlined WrestleMania X. He also won the tag titles with Owen Hart.

He died in 2000, at 34, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2012.
