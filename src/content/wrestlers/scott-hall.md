Scott Hall, born in Maryland, debuted in 1984 and worked the AWA and WCW, where he was briefly the Diamond Studd with Diamond Dallas Page. As Razor Ramon in the WWF he won the Intercontinental title four times, and his ladder match with Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania X changed what ladder matches could be.

In May 1996 he appeared on Nitro, threatening a "war" as one of the "Outsiders" with Kevin Nash, and with Hulk Hogan formed the nWo, the most influential angle of the Monday Night War. He won the WCW tag titles several times.

He battled addiction for years before DDP helped him get sober. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2014 and with the nWo in 2020, and he died in 2022.
