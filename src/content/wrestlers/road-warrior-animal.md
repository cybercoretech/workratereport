Joe Laurinaitis was a bouncer in Minneapolis when he was paired with Hawk in Georgia Championship Wrestling in 1983. With manager Paul Ellering, the Road Warriors became the most influential tag team ever and the only one to hold the AWA, NWA and WWF tag titles.

They won the first Crockett Cup in 1986, were part of the first WarGames, and as the Legion of Doom in the WWF won the tag titles at SummerSlam 1991. They were also huge stars in All Japan.

Animal kept the team going after Hawk's death in 2003 and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with Hawk and Ellering in 2011. He died in 2020.
