Kurt Angle was a two-time NCAA Division I heavyweight champion at Clarion University and won freestyle gold at the 1996 Olympics with a broken neck. He signed with the WWF in 1998 and debuted in 1999 as a goofy, patriotic "Olympic hero".

He won the European and Intercontinental titles, the King of the Ring and the WWF title in his first year, and his matches with Brock Lesnar, Shawn Michaels and Eddie Guerrero made him one of the best wrestlers of the 2000s. He was a multi-time WWE world champion.

In TNA from 2006 to 2016 he was its biggest star and a six-time world champion. He returned to WWE as Raw general manager in 2017, was inducted into the Hall of Fame the same year, and retired in 2019.
