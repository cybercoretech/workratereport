Dwayne Johnson, the son of Rocky Johnson and grandson of High Chief Peter Maivia, played college football at Miami and in the CFL. He wrestled briefly in the USWA as Flex Kavana and debuted in the WWF in 1996 as Rocky Maivia.

Fans booed the clean-cut rookie, so he turned heel, joined the Nation, and became The Rock. His catchphrases and feuds with Steve Austin, Mankind and Triple H made him one of the biggest stars ever, and he won the WWF/WWE title eight times.

He became one of the biggest movie stars in the world, with films grossing over $11 billion. He has returned to WWE part time, including WrestleMania matches with John Cena and, in 2024, as "The Final Boss".
