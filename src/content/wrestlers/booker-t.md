Booker Huffman broke into wrestling in Texas and formed Harlem Heat with his brother Lash (Stevie Ray). They won the WCW tag titles a record ten times, and Booker added a record six World Television titles, making him the most decorated wrestler in WCW history.

He became a five-time WCW World champion and was WCW's last World and US champion when the WWF bought the company. In WWE he won the World Heavyweight title as the regal "King Booker" in 2006, and he held 35 championships across WCW, the WWF/WWE and TNA.

He's a two-time WWE Hall of Famer, runs his own promotion and school, Reality of Wrestling, in Houston, and calls NXT as a commentator.
