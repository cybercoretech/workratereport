Arguably the best technical wrestler of his generation. As Bryan Danielson he was the first great Ring of Honor champion; as Daniel Bryan he led the "Yes!" movement to a WWE title win in the main event of WrestleMania XXX. He returned to the ring after a medical retirement and capped his career as AEW World champion in 2024.
