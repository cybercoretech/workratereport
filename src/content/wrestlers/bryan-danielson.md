Bryan Danielson, from Aberdeen, Washington, trained at Shawn Michaels' school and started wrestling in 1999. He had a WWF developmental deal from 2000 to 2003, but made his name in Ring of Honor from its first show in 2002, where his long ROH World title reign and his matches with Nigel McGuinness, KENTA and Takeshi Morishima set the standard for independent wrestling.

As Daniel Bryan in WWE, the "Yes!" movement made him one of the most popular wrestlers in the world. He won the WWE title in the main event of WrestleMania XXX, beating Triple H earlier the same night. Concussions forced him to retire in 2016, but he was cleared in 2018 and won the WWE title again.

He joined AEW in 2021, had a celebrated run of matches, and won the AEW World title at All In 2024. He now mostly calls Dynamite as a color commentator, with occasional matches.
