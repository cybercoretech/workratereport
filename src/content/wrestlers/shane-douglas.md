Troy Martin, from Pennsylvania, worked for Bill Watts' UWF and for WCW, where he won the tag titles with Ricky Steamboat. In ECW he won the NWA World title in a 1994 tournament, threw it down and declared himself ECW World champion, which turned Eastern Championship Wrestling into Extreme.

"The Franchise" won the ECW World title four times and holds the record for the most combined days as champion. He also had runs in the WWF and WCW.

He later wrestled in XPW and TNA, and became a promoter and manager.
