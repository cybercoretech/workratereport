Tommy Laughlin, from Yonkers, New York, joined ECW early and became its most loyal soldier, taking punishment week after week in storylines with Raven and the Sandman. He won the ECW World title in 2001.

In WWE from 2001 to 2010 he was a respected veteran and hardcore champion, and the only man to win the world title in both the original ECW and WWE's ECW.

He later ran his own promotion, House of Hardcore, and became a producer and commentator, mainly for TNA.
