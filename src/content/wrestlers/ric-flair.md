The Nature Boy, and the measuring stick for what a world champion is supposed to be. Flair carried the NWA title across the territories through the 1980s, led the Four Horsemen, and is credited with 16 world championships across the NWA, WCW and WWF. Few wrestlers have ever been better at making an opponent look like a star.
