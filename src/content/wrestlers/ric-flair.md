Richard Fliehr was adopted as a baby and grew up in Minnesota. He trained under Verne Gagne and debuted in the AWA in 1972. He survived a plane crash in 1975 that broke his back, came back the next year, and became the Mid-Atlantic's biggest star.

"The Nature Boy" won the NWA World title from Dusty Rhodes in 1981 and carried it across the territories and the world. He led the Four Horsemen, and his rivalries with Dusty, Ricky Steamboat, Sting, Harley Race and others defined the 1980s. He's credited with 16 world titles across the NWA, WCW and WWF.

Widely regarded as the greatest wrestler of all time, he wrestled until 2022 across 50 years, was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame twice, and is the father of Charlotte Flair. He has a legends deal with AEW.
