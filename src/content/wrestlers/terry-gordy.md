Terry Gordy started wrestling as a teenager and was the power of the Fabulous Freebirds, with Michael Hayes and Buddy Roberts, in Mid-South, Georgia and World Class, where their feud with the Von Erichs drew huge crowds.

In All Japan he became a star in his own right. He won the Triple Crown Heavyweight title and, with Steve Williams as the Miracle Violence Connection, the tag titles several times.

He died in 2001, at 40, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the Freebirds in 2016.
