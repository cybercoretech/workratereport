Bill Goldberg played football at the University of Georgia and in the NFL with the Atlanta Falcons before injuries ended his career. WCW signed him in 1997, and his long undefeated streak in singles matches made him one of the most popular figures of the late-1990s boom.

He beat Hollywood Hogan for the WCW World title in front of a huge crowd at the Georgia Dome on Nitro, also held the US title twice and the tag titles, became WCW's highest-paid wrestler, and stayed its franchise player until the company was sold in 2001.

In WWE he won the World Heavyweight title in 2003, then returned in 2016 to win the Universal title. He wrestled his retirement match in 2025 in Atlanta.
