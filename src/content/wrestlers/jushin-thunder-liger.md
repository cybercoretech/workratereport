Keiichi Yamada debuted for New Japan in 1984 and is the longest-tenured member of its roster. In 1989 he became Jushin Liger, a character based on a Go Nagai anime, and with his full bodysuit and mask he became the face of junior heavyweight wrestling.

He won the IWGP Junior Heavyweight title a record eleven times, created the Super J-Cup tournament, and his matches with Brian Pillman in WCW and with countless rivals in Japan helped spread the junior style worldwide.

He wrestled over 4,000 matches in 35 years, retired at Wrestle Kingdom 14 in 2020, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame the same year. He now trains wrestlers for New Japan.
