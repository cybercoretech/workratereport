Edward Moore started wrestling in 2002 and worked for Chikara, CZW, IWA Mid-South, Evolve, AAW, the NWA, Impact, PWG and even CMLL. He was a hard-hitting brawler with the most honest promos in the business.

AEW signed him in 2020, and his rivalry and friendship with Jon Moxley drove some of the company's most emotional television. In 2023 he won the ROH World title, the NJPW Strong Openweight title and the new AEW Continental title, defending all three together as the Continental Crown.

Beloved for wearing his heart, and his struggles with mental health, in the open, he's one of AEW's most authentic characters.
