Brock Lesnar won the NCAA Division I heavyweight title for the University of Minnesota in 2000 and signed with the WWF right after. He debuted on the main roster in 2002 as "The Next Big Thing" with Paul Heyman, won King of the Ring, and beat The Rock for the WWE title that August.

He left in 2004, had a tryout with the NFL's Minnesota Vikings, won the IWGP Heavyweight title in Japan, and then became UFC Heavyweight champion, the only person to win the top titles of WWE, the UFC, New Japan, IGF and the NCAA.

He returned to WWE in 2012 and became one of its biggest attractions: he ended the Undertaker's WrestleMania streak in 2014 and held the WWE and Universal titles several times. He retired from wrestling in 2026.
