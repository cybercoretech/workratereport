Robert Szatkowski trained under the Sheik and worked in WCW, the NWA and All Japan before ECW, where his kicks, flips and flexibility made him its most popular star. He held the ECW TV title for nearly 700 days.

He joined the WWF in 2001 during the Invasion and became a multi-time Intercontinental champion. In 2006 he cashed in Money in the Bank and beat John Cena for the WWE title in front of a hostile-to-Cena ECW crowd, and became ECW World champion too.

He later wrestled for TNA, AAA, Noah, AEW and MLW, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2021.
