Hossein Vaziri was a real amateur wrestler in Iran who came to the U.S. and helped coach the American team before turning pro in 1972. With his camel clutch and Persian clubs he became one of the great villains of the Cold War era.

He won the WWF title from Bob Backlund in December 1983, then lost it to Hulk Hogan four weeks later, the match that launched Hulkamania. With Nikolai Volkoff he won the WWF tag titles at the first WrestleMania.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2005, became a cult favorite again for his social media posts, and died in 2023.
