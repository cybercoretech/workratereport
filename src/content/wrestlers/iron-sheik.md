The Iron Sheik beat Bob Backlund for the WWF title in December 1983, then lost it to Hulk Hogan four weeks later in the match that launched Hulkamania. A real amateur wrestler, he was one of the great Cold War-era villains, especially teamed with Nikolai Volkoff.
