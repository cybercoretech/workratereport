Jeff Jarrett began wrestling in 1986 in his father Jerry Jarrett's Continental Wrestling Association in Memphis. He reached the WWF in 1993 as a country music wannabe and became a multi-time Intercontinental champion.

He moved back and forth between the WWF and WCW for nine years, winning the WCW World title, and was at the center of WCW's final year. In 2002 he and his father founded NWA: Total Nonstop Action, where he held the NWA World title six times.

He later founded Global Force Wrestling, worked in WWE as a producer, and joined AEW in 2022.
