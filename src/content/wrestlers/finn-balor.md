Fergal Devitt from Bray, Ireland, started wrestling in 2001 and made his name in New Japan as Prince Devitt. He was a three-time IWGP Junior Heavyweight champion, a six-time junior tag champion with Minoru and Ryusuke Taguchi, a two-time Best of the Super Juniors winner, and the founder and first leader of Bullet Club.

WWE signed him in 2014. As Finn Bálor he won the NXT title, and his body-painted "Demon" entrances became a signature. In 2016 he won the first Universal title in his first pay-per-view on the main roster, but a shoulder injury forced him to give it up the next night.

He later won the Intercontinental title, returned to NXT as champion, and found new life leading the Judgment Day.
