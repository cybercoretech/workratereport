Jim Hellwig was a bodybuilder before wrestling. He started in 1985 as one of the Blade Runners with Sting, then became the Dingo Warrior in World Class.

In the WWF his intensity and energy made him a phenomenon. He won the Intercontinental title in under a minute at SummerSlam 1988, and at WrestleMania VI he beat Hulk Hogan for the WWF title.

He had brief returns in 1992, 1996 and WCW in 1998. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in April 2014 and died three days later.
