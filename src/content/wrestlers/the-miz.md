Mike Mizanin first became famous on MTV's The Real World and Road Rules Challenge before signing with WWE in 2004. He worked his way up through Tough Enough and developmental, and won the tag titles with John Morrison.

He won the US title and Money in the Bank, and in 2010 cashed in to become WWE champion, beating John Cena in the main event of WrestleMania XXVII. He later won the WWE title again in 2021, and his 2016 Intercontinental title feuds made him one of WWE's best talkers.

He's an eight-time Intercontinental champion and a Grand Slam champion, and he hosts TV shows and his own reality series.
