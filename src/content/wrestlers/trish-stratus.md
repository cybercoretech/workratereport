Patricia Stratigeas studied at York University and worked as a fitness model before the WWF signed her in 2000 as a manager. She trained hard and became the best woman in the company.

She won the WWF/WWE Women's title seven times, and her 448-day reign is the longest of any women's world champion this century. Her feud with Lita headlined Raw in 2004, and she retired in 2006 after beating Lita in Toronto.

She was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2013 and has made several comebacks.
