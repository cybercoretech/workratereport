Mark Pugh grew up on a Delaware farm with his brother Jay, and they started wrestling as teenagers. As the Briscoe Brothers they won the ROH World tag titles a record 13 times, and Mark was known for his wild, fearless dives.

After Jay's death in January 2023, Mark carried on alone, wrestling emotional tribute matches and becoming ROH World champion himself.

In AEW he leads the Conglomeration and has won the TNT title.
