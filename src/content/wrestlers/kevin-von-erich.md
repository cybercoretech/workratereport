Kevin Adkisson is Fritz Von Erich's second son and the eldest to survive childhood. In his father's World Class Championship Wrestling he wrestled barefoot, flying around the ring in the Von Erichs' battles with the Fabulous Freebirds that drew huge crowds to the Sportatorium and Texas Stadium.

He held the WCWA World Heavyweight title and many tag and six-man titles with his brothers. After the deaths of David, Mike, Chris and Kerry, he was the last surviving Von Erich brother, and the story was told in the 2023 film The Iron Claw.

He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the family in 2009, and his sons Marshall and Ross wrestle today. He signed with AEW as a legend and coach.
