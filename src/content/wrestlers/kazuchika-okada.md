"The Rainmaker" defined New Japan in the 2010s. A five-time IWGP Heavyweight champion, with a record 720-day reign from 2016 to 2018, his matches with Kenny Omega and Hiroshi Tanahashi set the modern standard. He joined AEW in 2024.
