Kazuchika Okada started wrestling in Mexico's Toryumon at 16, joined New Japan in 2007, and was sent on excursion to TNA in 2010. He came back in 2012 as the cocky "Rainmaker", beat Tanahashi for the IWGP title, and the two became the company's defining rivalry.

He's a five-time IWGP Heavyweight champion, and his 720-day reign from 2016 to 2018 is a record. His Wrestle Kingdom 11 match with Kenny Omega was the first six-star match in Observer history, and he won the G1 Climax multiple times.

He joined AEW in 2024 as part of the Don Callis Family. He set the record for the longest Continental title reign (647 days), became the only AEW Unified champion, and has held the International title.
