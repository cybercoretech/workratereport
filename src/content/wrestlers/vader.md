Leon White played football at Colorado and briefly with the Los Angeles Rams before wrestling in the AWA. In 1987 he became Big Van Vader in New Japan, where he won the IWGP Heavyweight title three times.

In WCW he won the world title three times, and his wars with Sting and Cactus Jack are legendary. He also held the CWA title in Austria and the Triple Crown in All Japan, and he's widely regarded as the greatest super-heavyweight ever.

In the WWF he main-evented SummerSlam 1996. He died in 2018.
