Chris Irvine trained in Calgary at the Hart Brothers' school and wrestled around the world in the early 1990s, in Canada, Mexico, Japan, ECW and then WCW, where he was a cruiserweight standout with a gift for comedy.

He jumped to the WWF in 1999 as Y2J. In December 2001 he beat The Rock and Steve Austin on the same night to become the first Undisputed WWF champion. He went on to win world titles several more times in WWE, and his 2008 feud with Shawn Michaels was one of the best of the decade. He also fronts the rock band Fozzy.

In 2019 he became AEW's first world champion, and his Wrestle Kingdom matches with Kenny Omega and others kept him relevant into his fifties.
