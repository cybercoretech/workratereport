James Fullington started in Tod Gordon's Eastern Championship Wrestling in 1992 and became the Sandman, a cane-swinging, beer-drinking everyman whose long entrance through the crowd became an ECW ritual.

He won the ECW World title a record five times, and his storylines with Raven and Tommy Dreamer were among the most emotional in ECW. He also worked in WCW, TNA and WWE's ECW revival.

He retired in April 2026 with a comedy match against the Invisible Man at Joey Janela's Spring Break.
