The son of Dusty Rhodes and the half-brother of Dustin, Cody Runnels won two Georgia state amateur titles before signing with WWE in 2006. He won every title in Ohio Valley Wrestling, then won seven tag titles and two Intercontinental titles in WWE, including a run as the cosmic Stardust.

He asked for his release in 2016, became "The American Nightmare" on the independents and in ROH and New Japan, and with the Young Bucks staged All In in 2018. That led to AEW, which he co-founded as an executive vice president in 2019.

He returned to WWE at WrestleMania 38 in 2022, won the Royal Rumble in 2023 and 2024, and at WrestleMania XL beat Roman Reigns to "finish the story" his father never could and become Undisputed WWE champion.
