"The Snake" was one of the most psychologically gifted performers in wrestling: a quiet, menacing talker with the DDT and a python named Damien. He was a featured star in the WWF from 1986 to 1992 and again in 1996, when his feud with Steve Austin helped launch "Austin 3:16".
