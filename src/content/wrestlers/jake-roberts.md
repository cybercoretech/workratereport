Aurelian Smith Jr., the son of wrestler Grizzly Smith, wrestled across the territories, including Mid-South and the NWA, and is credited with popularizing the DDT. He was known as one of the best talkers ever, quiet, intense and psychological.

In the WWF from 1986 to 1992 he feuded with Ricky Steamboat, Rick Rude, Randy Savage and the Undertaker. He returned in 1996, and his King of the Ring final against Steve Austin led directly to the "Austin 3:16" promo.

He struggled for years with addiction until DDP's help and DDP Yoga turned his life around in the 2010s. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2014 and later appeared in AEW.
