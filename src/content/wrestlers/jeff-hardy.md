Jeff Hardy grew up in Cameron, North Carolina, where he and his brother Matt ran backyard shows before their own promotion, OMEGA. As the Hardy Boyz in the WWF they helped revive tag wrestling with the TLC matches.

"The Charismatic Enigma" became a singles star, won the WWE and World Heavyweight titles in 2008–09, and later won the TNA World title.

He had runs with WWE and AEW in the 2010s and 2020s, has dealt with addiction, and is back in TNA, where the Hardys have won the tag titles five times.
