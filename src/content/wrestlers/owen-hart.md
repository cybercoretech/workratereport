Bret's brother and one of the most gifted wrestlers of the 1990s. Owen beat Bret in the opening match of WrestleMania X, won the 1994 King of the Ring, held the Intercontinental and tag titles, and was famous for his humor. He died in an accident at Over the Edge 1999.
