Owen Hart, the youngest of Stu and Helen Hart's twelve children, grew up in Calgary and wrestled in Stampede Wrestling, New Japan and WCW, where his high-flying skill impressed everyone. He first came to the WWF in 1988 as the masked Blue Blazer.

His 1994 feud with Bret, starting with his win at WrestleMania X, made him a top heel. He won the Intercontinental title twice, the tag titles with the British Bulldog and Yokozuna, and was known backstage as one of the funniest people in wrestling.

He was also a USWA Unified World champion. He died on May 23, 1999, after a fall from a stunt harness at Over the Edge.
