Hiroshi Tanahashi debuted for New Japan in 1999. In the mid-2000s, when MMA had pulled fans away and the company was struggling, "The Ace" became its savior with a rock-star persona and a modern style.

He held a record eight IWGP Heavyweight title reigns and 26 New Japan championships in all, including the IWGP US and Intercontinental titles, and won the G1 Climax three times. His feuds with Shinsuke Nakamura, Kazuchika Okada and Kenny Omega defined the company's revival in the 2010s.

He became New Japan's president in 2023 and wrestled his retirement match in 2026.
