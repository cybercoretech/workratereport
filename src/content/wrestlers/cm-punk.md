Phil Brooks started wrestling on the independents in 1997 and joined Ring of Honor in 2002. His outspoken straight-edge persona, based on his real life, and his trilogy with Samoa Joe made him an ROH legend and ROH World champion.

WWE signed him in 2005. He won the ECW title and two Money in the Bank ladder matches, and became a three-time World Heavyweight champion. His 2011 "pipe bomb" promo and his WWE title win over John Cena in Chicago led to a 434-day reign, and he won the Intercontinental title too.

He walked out of WWE in 2014, fought in the UFC, returned to wrestling with AEW in 2021, where he won its world title twice, and came back to WWE in November 2023.
