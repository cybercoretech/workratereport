Mark Calaway started in 1987 in World Class and other territories under several names, and wrestled in WCW in 1989–90 as Mean Mark Callous. The WWF debuted him at Survivor Series 1990 as the Undertaker.

Over three decades he won the world title seven times, built a 21–0 WrestleMania streak, and had legendary matches with Shawn Michaels, Mick Foley, Triple H, Kane and many more. He also had a biker phase as the "American Badass".

He retired after WrestleMania 36 in 2020 and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2022. He's a WWE ambassador and works on the creative side of AAA.
