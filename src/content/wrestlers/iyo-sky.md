Masami Odate wrestled as Io Shirai and became the cornerstone of World Wonder Ring Stardom: a two-time World of Stardom champion, a two-time Wonder of Stardom champion and a six-time Artist of Stardom champion.

She signed with WWE in 2018. In NXT she won the NXT Women's title and the Women's tag titles with Zoey Stark. As Iyo Sky on the main roster, she joined Damage CTRL and cashed in Money in the Bank in 2023 to win the WWE Women's title.

She has since held women's titles on Raw and won the Queen of the Ring tournament.
