Steve Williams was a four-time All-American wrestler and a football player at Oklahoma. Bill Watts brought him into Mid-South, where he won the UWF World title, and he became one of the most feared men in wrestling.

In All Japan he formed the Miracle Violence Connection with Terry Gordy, winning the tag titles several times, and he also won the Triple Crown. In the U.S. he won tag titles in the NWA and WCW.

He fought cancer in his later years and died in 2009.
