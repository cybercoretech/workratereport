Jason Reso started wrestling in 1995 in Canada, teaming with his lifelong best friend Adam Copeland (Edge). WWF signed him in 1998, and Edge & Christian won the tag titles seven times, starring in the Tables, Ladders and Chairs matches with the Hardys and Dudleys.

As a singles wrestler he won the Intercontinental title several times. In TNA as Christian Cage he was NWA World champion, and back in WWE he finally won the World Heavyweight title in 2011.

He joined AEW in 2021, won the Impact World title and the TNT title, became one of the best heels in the company, and reunited with Copeland as Cage & Cope.
