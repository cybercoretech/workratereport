Scott Levy worked as Scotty the Body and Scotty Flamingo, and as Johnny Polo in the WWF, before reinventing himself in ECW in 1995 as Raven, a brooding grunge outcast with a Flock of followers.

His storylines with Tommy Dreamer and the Sandman were among the most emotional in wrestling. He won the ECW World title twice, then led the Flock in WCW and won the US title.

In TNA he won the NWA World title in 2005. He's regarded as one of the best talkers in wrestling history.
