Paul Wight played college basketball before starting to wrestle in 1994. WCW signed him in 1995 as The Giant, and within months he was world champion, later running with the nWo.

He jumped to the WWF in 1999 as Big Show and spent more than two decades there as a monster heel, a sympathetic giant and everything in between. He's a seven-time world champion across the WCW, WWF/WWE and ECW titles, and he held 23 championships in all.

He moved to AEW in 2021 under his real name, as a wrestler and later a commentator, and along the way acted in film and TV.
