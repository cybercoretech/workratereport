Amy Dumas started wrestling in 1998, training and performing in Mexico for CMLL as Angelica, then worked the U.S. independents and briefly ECW. The WWF signed her in 1999 and she debuted as Lita in 2000.

With the Hardy Boyz in Team Xtreme she brought high-flying moves rarely seen from women. She won the WWF/WWE Women's title four times, and her rivalry with Trish Stratus main-evented Raw in 2004.

She retired in 2006 and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2014, and she later made comebacks in 2022 and 2023.
