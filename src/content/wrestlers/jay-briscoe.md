Jamin Pugh and his younger brother Mark grew up on a farm in Delaware and started wrestling as teenagers. As the Briscoe Brothers they became the backbone of Ring of Honor, winning the ROH World tag titles a record 13 times.

Jay was also a two-time ROH World champion and an ROH Six-Man champion, and the Briscoes held the Impact and IWGP tag titles. Their 2022 series with FTR was considered some of the best tag wrestling of the decade.

He was inducted into the ROH Hall of Fame in 2022, and he died in a car accident in January 2023, at 38.
