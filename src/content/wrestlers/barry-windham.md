The son of Blackjack Mulligan and the brother-in-law of Mike Rotunda, Barry Windham was one of the most gifted big men of the 1980s, smooth enough to wrestle like a junior heavyweight. He and Rotunda won the WWF tag titles as the U.S. Express.

In Florida and the NWA he became Ric Flair's best regular challenger, then turned heel in 1988 to join the Four Horsemen as US champion. In all he held the NWA World title once, the US title, the TV title and the NWA/WCW tag titles four times.

He beat the Great Muta for the NWA World title in 1993. He's in the WWE Hall of Fame twice: with the Four Horsemen in 2012 and with the U.S. Express in 2024.
