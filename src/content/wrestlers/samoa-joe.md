Joe Seanoa debuted in 1999 and joined ROH soon after its 2002 launch. His 645-day World title reign from 2003 to 2004, his trilogy with CM Punk and his match with Kenta Kobashi made him one of the best wrestlers in the world.

In TNA he went undefeated for over a year, had legendary X Division and main event matches with AJ Styles, Christopher Daniels and Kurt Angle, and became TNA World champion.

In WWE he won the NXT title twice and the US title. He joined AEW in 2022, won the TNT and ROH TV titles, then the AEW World title, and he leads the Opps.
