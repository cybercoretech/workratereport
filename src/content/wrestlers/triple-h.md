Paul Levesque started in 1992 as Terra Ryzing and worked WCW in 1994 as the French aristocrat Jean-Paul Lévesque. In the WWF from 1995 he was the blue-blooded Hunter Hearst Helmsley, and he became Triple H in DX.

From 1999 he was the company's top heel, and his matches with Mick Foley, The Rock, Steve Austin and Shawn Michaels defined the era. He's a 14-time world champion and led Evolution in the 2000s.

He created NXT, became WWE's head of creative in 2022 and its chief content officer, and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2019 with DX.
